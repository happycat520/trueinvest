About CrystalWell

Built to surface what's already public.

Investing doesn't have to mean chasing the hottest stocks or predicting tomorrow's headlines — sometimes the data already tells you where to look.

CrystalWell Analytics was built on a simple idea: a lot of genuinely useful information is already public — insider buying and selling disclosed in SEC Form 4 filings, dividend and earnings calendars, and multi-day price action — it's just scattered and easy to miss. We track it daily across the DJIA and S&P 500 and deliver it as structured signals: a live dashboard and a daily digest, sourced directly from SEC EDGAR and market data.

Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. We deliver the data and the context around it — what you do with it is up to you.

For readers who want the reasoning behind how we built these signals — the philosophy and process we use to decide what's worth tracking — that's covered separately in our Framework.

If tracking this data yourself saves you time, or helps you catch something you'd have otherwise missed, we've done our job.
